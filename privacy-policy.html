1. Overview & Core Privacy Principle

Digital Tasbeeh is an offline-first mobile application designed with your privacy as the top priority.
  • We do not collect any personal information.
  • We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any personal details.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

When you download, install, or use Digital Tasbeeh, we do not collect, process, or transmit:

  • Personal Identifiers: No names, email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, or account credentials.
  • Location Data: No precise or coarse GPS location data.
  • Financial Information: No payment details, credit card numbers, or transaction histories (the App is completely free).
  • Device Hardware & Files: No access to your contact list, camera, microphone, photo gallery, media files, or external documents.
  • Tracking Identifiers: No Google Advertising ID (AAID), IDFA, IMEI, MAC address, or persistent device fingerprinting.

3. On-Device (Local) Data Storage

All data created or modified while using the App is stored strictly locally on your device in the App's secure private storage sandbox using Hive (a lightweight, local database):

  • Custom Wazifas and Azkar: Any custom prayers, names, translations, or target counts you add.
  • Counting Progress & History: Your completed counts, round numbers, and session timestamps.
  • Application Settings: Your preferences for vibration feedback, audio clicks, display theme (light/dark mode), and interface language.

This data never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any remote server or cloud database.

4. Local Notifications and Reminders

If you choose to enable daily, weekly, or custom reminder notifications within the App:

  • Reminders are managed and triggered entirely on your device via the Android system's local alarm scheduler and the flutter_local_notifications package.
  • No push notification servers, remote tokens, or external messaging platforms (such as Firebase Cloud Messaging or OneSignal) are utilized.
  • Reminder notification times and scheduled days remain stored exclusively on your device.

5. Third-Party Libraries and SDKs

To provide standard application functionality, the App uses reputable, open-source Flutter packages:

  • Flutter Framework (Google LLC): Core cross-platform UI framework.
  • audioplayers: Local playback of audio click sounds for counting feedback.
  • vibration: Local device haptic feedback when counting buttons are pressed.
  • flutter_local_notifications: On-device local alarm scheduling for dhikr reminders.
  • in_app_review: Standard Google Play in-app review API to request a store rating, operating strictly via official Google Play APIs without collecting personal user information.

None of these packages collect, store, or transmit your personal data to external servers or third-party analytics platforms.

6. Data Retention, User Rights, and Data Deletion

Because all application data is stored exclusively on your local device:

  • Data Retention: Your counting history and settings are retained on your device for as long as the App remains installed.
  • Data Deletion Options:
    1. Reset In-App: You can reset any individual dhikr count or history directly within the counter screen using the "Reset" button.
    2. Clear App Data: You can delete all data at any time via your device settings: Settings > Apps > Digital Tasbeeh > Storage > Clear Data (or Clear Storage).
    3. Uninstall the App: Uninstalling Digital Tasbeeh from your device permanently and automatically removes all locally stored data, databases, and preferences.
  • No Server-Side Deletion Request Required: Because BrotherCoders does not store, transmit, or retain any of your data on external servers or cloud infrastructure, there is no remote server-side record to delete.
