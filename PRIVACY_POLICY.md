# Privacy Policy for Digital Tasbeeh

**Last updated:** August 30, 2026

## Introduction

Digital Tasbeeh ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Do Not Collect

Digital Tasbeeh is designed with privacy in mind. **We do not collect any personal information.**

- We do not require you to create an account.
- We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal identifiers.
- We do not track your location.
- We do not access your contacts, photos, or files.

## Information Stored Locally

All data created within the app (such as your custom wazifas, dhikr progress, and app settings) is stored **locally on your device** using Hive, a lightweight local database. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly back it up through your device's own backup mechanisms.

## Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, the app uses your device's local notification system. All reminder scheduling is handled locally on your device. We do not send push notifications from any external server.

## Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party packages for functionality:
- **Flutter framework** (by Google)
- **audioplayers** for sound effects
- **flutter_local_notifications** for local reminders
- **in_app_review** for app store rating prompts

These packages operate entirely within the app and do not transmit personal data to external servers.

## Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. Since we do not collect any personal information, there is no data to protect in this regard.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
